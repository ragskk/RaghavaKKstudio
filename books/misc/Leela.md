# Leela

*Memorial booklet, A5 (family, 2022)*

A tribute booklet for Leela, 1957 to 2021, dated July 3rd, 2022. Twenty-eight pages, image only: the Canva export carries no text layer, so the pages are photographs and set type reproduced as pictures. The cover is a portrait of Leela with her name in neon script; the opening pages name a star in Orion after her and show where to find it.
