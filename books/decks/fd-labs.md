## Slide 5 — What and How?

- FD Labs + Dhun + OS + Lara launch a stable coin called C3
- C3 is always equal to 1 Carbon credit
- FD will facilitate (for a small transaction fee) the purchase of C3’s from Dhun at market value
- Dhun will use the process to
- Grow asset/property (~80%)
- Enable co benefits - (20%) including art/ community/ research/ education etc.
- Artists and curators etc who participate in the first launch get founder coin allocations
- Holders of C3 may keep their asset in crypto or trade it/ stake it.
- Artists will use this funding to create a series of NFT’s for The Launch of the Dhunn Project on Opensea.
- NFT’s can only be purchased using C3 will will be available for purchase via $$ or CC’s via FD Eco and CoinBase etc.
- Upon sale of the NFT - automatic split (smart contract initiates)
  - Artists (30%) , Dhun (30%), Curator (10%), Lara (30%)
- Dhun splits its income as always (80% grow asset; 20% artist collective)
