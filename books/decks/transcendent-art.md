## Slide 2 — 2021

- Transcendent Art is a designed or intended encounter with an art object for transcendence (mystical growth).

## Slide 3 — 2021

- Transcendent art for us is art that elicits a relationship between the viewer and the artwork, through a designed encounter, with the intention of growing spiritually (intellectually and viscerally).
- An encounter with t-art can inspire:
- Growth (loss, liberation, mystery) *see thesis
- Possibility (non-deterministic)
- Hope (exposure to alternate realities)
- Inclusivity (power dynamics between the object and viewer)
- Personality (dynamic and changes unlike a brand)
- Vivacity (feeling alive/ operating From Joy vs For Joy)
- Religion is art taken very seriously! - Gino

## Slide 4 — 2021

- Individually each artwork is a lived personal conflict. This is called personality. Therefore all personalities put together creates always to resolve binaries
- Since every concept has to differentiate itself from one another, they create a natural binary tension with each other, causing an endless loop of infinite conceptual binary‘s. This forms a web of conceptual possibilities. Now add the concepts manifest conflict to this equation and mathematically you end up with infinite possibilities.
- This realization of infinite possibilities is not graspable by our own brains or our bodies. We can only be made aware of its existence true: coincidences ; rational conclusions ; feeling connected and emergence.
- Because we cannot grasp infinite possibilities, we either reject it or assign too much value to it.
- When rejected, we arrive at Nihilism. The state of nihilism kills vivacity -the quality of being attractively lively and animated.
- The antidote to nihilism is personality.
- The Personal resolution of binaries using one's own response mechanisms to the environment.
- So how does one find their own personal resolution of binaries?
