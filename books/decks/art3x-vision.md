## Slide 3 — 2021

- It is March 2020 and the world has been disrupted like never before. Particularly the art world, which is the first to suffer an economic hit in a recession, is in panic as the world comes to a grinding halt.
- In early 2020, Museums started cancelling commissions, galleries could not make rent, Theaters doors remained shut for what seems like forever, collectors and patrons can no longer justify enormous spending on art, when people are losing jobs, homes and loved ones.
- The art world for us includes the new cutting edge avant garde creators including
- painters, sculptors, AI artists, rappers, dancers, bio hackers, bitcoin artists, VR sculptors, video game artists, performance artists, utopia artists, psychedelic chefs etc.)

## Slide 5 — 2021

- The ultimate artist is believed to be one who can create his/her own reality. The greatest artists in history were polymaths. They worked with science, chemistry (to create pigments), biology ( to study anatomy) and also engaged deeply with philosophy and government. In short they took art beyond the gallery into the world at large. Where are those people now?
- Meet the people who dare to imagine alternate realities for us. They were born with a crazy belief in a way of life and they would go against the grain to create small pockets of an alternate world. They live life on their terms, they wear their personalities on their shoulders. These reality benders are unlike the heroes we know. They reinvent themselves, they cross boundaries, they celebrate their failures, and most importantly, live their own truths. In doing so, they give us glimpses of alternate ways of living. They give humanity hope when everything around us is falling apart.
- “Art has the most influential power of all, the power to inspire change!” - Raghava KK

## Slide 6 — 2021

I. Art should engage the whole artist

- The whole self for us consists of
- personal + professional + spiritual
- The artist should go beyond the gallery to engage with the world at large.
- The artist should be an active part of society and build community.
- The artist is open to sharing and learning from each other.

## Slide 9 — 2021

IV. Artists should imagine utopias and dystopias

Using new tools of transcendence of our times, we want artists to imagine alternate realities. When everything around us is falling apart, they give humanity a glimpse of hope of an alternate reality. Art has the most important function of all - to inspire and move.

## Slide 10 — 2021

V. Art should be future facing

- Most of art today is backward facing - caught up with identity politics.
- The art of the future focuses more on where we want to go as opposed to who we are. We are where we want to go.
- The role of the artist is to help society imagine and create the next best version of ourselves.

## Slide 11 — 2021

VI. Art as an attitude towards binaries

- We wish to offer the world an Eastern aesthetic approaches to contemporary art
- West - great at identifying binaries.
- East - great at confusing binaries.
- West + East —> attitude towards binaries
