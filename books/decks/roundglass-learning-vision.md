## Slide 25 — Fundamental questions

- What is art?
- What are the uses of art?
- What happens if we don't express ourselves?
- How is art different from science?
- How powerful can art get?
- Can art make people change?
- What makes people change?

- Why do we grow old?
- Why do we die?
- What happens to us when we die?
- How do people who are dieing think?
- How do we die?
- How long do people live?
- Where do people go when they die?

- Can healthy food ever be tasty?
- Why is sugar so bad?
- Why do we have so many desserts then?
- Why do people make unhealthy food?
- Why do I crave unhealthy food?
- Does color play a role in healthy food?
- Can food evoke memories?
- Eating with people vs eating alone?

- Who are you? What makes you you?
- Why are we different from each other?
- What am I not?
- What are my fears? Are fears good?

## Slide 27 — Measure

- Each project is measured against:
- SCALABILITY: Reach and impact? (People) (1-5)
AWE/DESIRABILITY: How desirable is to the audience? (Interest) (1-5)
STICKINESS: Engage and share(1-5)
FEASIBLE: What will it take to execute? (Resource) (Low/Medium/High)
AFFORDABILITY: What is the estimate cost $ (0-15k) / $$ (15 - 35k) / $$$ (35k+)
ALIGNMENT: How much does it align with the vision and element (vision) (1-5)
