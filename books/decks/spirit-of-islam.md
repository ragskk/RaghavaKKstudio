## Slide 6 — The beginning

- At immigration, each visitor to Abu Dhabi receives a unique, interactive, passport-sized book containing a treasure map of Abu Dhabi,
- The book serves two purposes: it a reference guide to learn about Abu Dhabi’s values and the “treasures” and a means to document memories and reflections from a visitor’s journey.
- The book itself is a soulful work of art. This way it differentiates itself clearly from a travel guide or a tourism pamphlet. The book is treated as sacred and will contain no advertising or sponsorship.
- The book contains a commemorative coin and an interactive map that changes color and reveals additional information someone scratches out a visited destination.

## Slide 8 — Experiencing Sadaqah(Charity, Generosity & Kindness)

- “Every act of goodness is Sadaqah.”
- Value: 
The spirit of generosity, kindness and giving
- Art: 
A flower sculpture that opens when people are caring and generous
- Experience:
- Residents and visitors participate to give through a website. Each petal stands for a specific cause/charity category and the more that is donated to that cause, the more that particular petal opens. The full flower opens when the community’s total fundraising goal is met, with an accompanying celebration.
- The installation is surrounded by water and there are music and lighting components which can change over time and during special occasions when the flower opens (e.g. New Year's Eve ball dropping in New York City).
- The full flower can also be opened by making a donation of a larger amount on-site via a donation booth or app/website. Donations can be dedicated to someone and directed to specific causes or charities.

## Slide 10 — The Adhan – The Call of the Conscience

- Value: 
Making Islam relatable to both believers and non-believers through the call of oneness. A call to gather oneself - we may come from different parts of the world but the sound unites us
- Art: 
A wind sculpture that opens at given times and sings with the wind
- Experience:
- There is a sacredness, stillness and silent awareness when the wind sculpture is turned on at special times
- There is a royal association, with the royal household holding the keys to it as well as determining the right times and recipients for this to be a gift to other nations.
- The idea behind gifting it is to reinforce how sound can unite us, how Islam stands for Peace. So there is an Adhan happening in every time zone across the world at any given time.

## Slide 12 — Experiencing Tawheed (Oneness) through Sacred Geometry & calligraphy

- Value: 
Experiencing
- Oneness through Sacred Geometry & calligraphy and
- Frugality - light has the power to transform buildings
- Art: 
A small, frugal sculpture with a powerful light source transforming the entire landscape. Shadow and light, seeming opposites, come together to create a magical world and an immersive experience. The sculpture features calligraphy of words that have significance in Islam and that are translated into other languages or sacred islamic geometry.

Experience:
- The sculpture has six faces that are removable and changeable, bringing freshness to the sculpture on a regular basis.
- This installation will travel across Abu Dhabi and other places and can change as it travels.
- It invites interaction - one side of the cube has a blank frame that invites viewers to create their own shadow art. Props can be provided near the installation and workshops can be offered in the community to play with shadows.
- A hashtag is used to encourage social media posts across the world and show what viewers have created. There can be contests and giveaways

## Slide 14 — Experiencing Symmetry and Order through Islamic geometry

- Value: 
Experiencing Symmetry and Order through Islamic geometry 

Art:
- A beautiful outdoor labyrinth which encourages a journey to one’s center rather than seeking a way out of a maze
- Experience:
- A labyrinth constructed with a question for reflection at every turn and walls full or art and poetry, possibly with augmented/virtual reality components and accompanying music. The entry-way is through a labyrinth of mirrors to help discern the difference between illusion and the truth and then take a journey to one’s center.
- The labyrinth gets tougher and tougher as you go closer to the center. Each traveler to the center performs a ritual there – leaving behind something they would like to shed by offering it into an urn at the center of the labyrinth.

## Slide 16 — Walls of Jihaad – striving for a personal promise

- “Declare your jihaad on thirteen enemies you cannot see - egoism, arrogance, conceit, selfishness, greed, lust, intolerance, anger, lying, cheating, gossiping and slandering. If you can master and destroy them, then you will be ready to fight the enemy you can see.”
- -Al Ghazali
- Value: 
(Jihad - fighting an inner struggle - what are you willing to let go of to do the right thing. Physical, Spirual, Moral and Mental effort.) Self Discipline - A promise to stand for/by something
- Art: 
Larger than life, fun, awe-inspiring murals across Abu Dhabi called the “Walls of Jihaad – striving for a personal promise/commitment.
- Experience:
- Abu Dhabi signature, Selfie walls where participants take a picture against a mural that inspires them to strive for something larger and stand by a promise they make to themselves.
- Hashtags and social media posts to make a public promise of their own personal Jihaad
- The murals contain sections with –
  - Blank spaces and interesting questions where people can express their views or Parts of the murals could be a question for people to chalk in their answer
  - Papers to write your wishes/promises and put into holes in the wall
