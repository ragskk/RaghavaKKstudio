## Slide 18

The small- big differences that make all the difference!

Networking vs

- Community building
- Done with intention
- Conscious or unconscious score erased
- Individual and Systemic benefit (or taking turns)
- Pay it forward → give when you have and take when you need.
- Now or later?
- Gratitude or disappointment - but zero entitlement
- Rules and Laws offer safety net
- Reward comes from the existence and nurturing of the community
- Surprises are welcomed but considered

- Done with agenda
- Conscious or unconscious score kept
- Individual benefit or system benefit
- Transaction → give <,>,= take
- Win or lose
- Entitlement due to “rules”
- Rules/ Laws dictate
- Rewards comes in tangible forms
- Avoid surprises
