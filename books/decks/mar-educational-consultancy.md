GIT-HUB

MYTH

Be nice and do good by open-sourcing. Content is atomic. Content has no history or structure. Photographing someone does not cause their soul to be stolen. Similarly, cloning the master repository does not diminish its importance.

ART

Fork and pull model of collaboration. GitFlow. Language, code of ethics (bible)

RITUAL

The Forked code- Make someone's code better. The gentle bump. Secrets, secrets, are no fun - no email, all open chat with cross reference links. Pull requests are community property.
