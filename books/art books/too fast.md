# too fast

Raghava KK 2026

I am the child in these paintings.

We learn very early that we are loved if we perform.

Be pleasant and useful. Be good at something. Do it again.

I learned it well. I thought it was love. It wasn't love. It was survival.

Now I open my phone and there they are.

Kids singing to a camera. Kids dancing for strangers.

I don't think the internet did this to them.

## I think this is the oldest deal there is, finally out in the open, and going too fast.

These boys are doing private things in public. That is the whole picture.

It's entertaining. It's sad. Both.

I'm not going to pick. Neither are you.
