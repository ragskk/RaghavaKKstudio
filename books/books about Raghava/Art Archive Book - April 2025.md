# Art Archive Book

Art Archive, 1997 to 2025. Raghava KK Studio, 2025.

Archive of the evolution of Raghava's artwork from 1997. Each section (letter of the alphabet) represents a shift in the direction of Raghava's art.

Each work is shown twice: once as a small catalogue image with its label and caption, and once as a full-page detail. The running header "RAGHAVA KK" appears on every work page. Works are labelled by section letter and number. Dimensions are height x width as printed; a year is given only where the book prints one.
