# Ceramics

*Studio catalogue of ceramic sculptures (2025); image only, no printed text*

This catalogue runs to 17 pages and carries photographs only; there is no printed text on any page and no text layer in the PDF. The pages show ceramic sculptures from 2025, glazed figures in pastel colours including lions, elephants, an archer, and a horse and rider. No titles, dimensions or captions are printed, so none are given here.
