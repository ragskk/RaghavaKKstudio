How the series made each other.

A map of cause, not a list of dates. The line is time. The framed pictures on it are the series, in the order I made them. Above the line is what happened in my life. Below it, what happened in my work. Every arrow says this fed that, and every arrow runs forward. Nothing feeds the past. What came earlier informs what came later; what came later can only answer.

The board opens on a wider screen. On a phone you get the ledger: the same arrows, written out.

How to read it.

Start anywhere. Rest on a framed picture and everything it touched lights up while the rest of the board fades. Click to hold it: what fed it turns black, what it fed turns red, and a card opens with both written out. Follow a slip or a dot the same way. Press Esc to let go. On a phone you get the ledger below instead, the same arrows written out.
