ProcessStudio · The Dossiers

Every series invents its own process.

There is no method here. Each body of work begins by building the process that will produce it: its own roles, its own exercises, its own rules. When the series ends, the process is abandoned with it. These dossiers record them, one by one, honestly: only the series whose making was documented as it happened.

Figure & Edges.

Six months, one apparatus, three words. A critic-brother sets exercises; the maker paints his first oils; a small diptych arrives that nobody asked for, and the impasto edge of the painting becomes the painting. Two series born of one journey; the page turns dark when the canvases do.

№ 022019 → 2020

The Toy Trojan.

The frame had to come out of a single piece of wood, so the work began in a forest. Thirty people, months of searching, a three-hundred-year-old mahogany, a ritual asking permission before the cut, three kilometres out on foot. Then a horse, carved, and a surface into which the whole present was written.

The Orgasm Project.

The artist's orgasm, recorded as brainwave data, visualised neuron by neuron, and returned to oil on canvas by a robot and a painter working together. Five steps: idea, data, visualisation, interpretation, matter. It ends at Sotheby's HQ, New York.
