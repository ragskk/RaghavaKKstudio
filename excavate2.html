A note before the catalogue

Collaborations sit alongside the studio's solo work. Nineteen are recorded below, plus the 64/1 collective as its own sister section. Ordered by impact for the viewer rather than by date. Each entry is still individually dated.

Each was made with an institution, a company, or a researcher. The form varied. A constant since 2015 is the studio's working method, machine-assisted underpainting finished by hand.

Every entry lists the client, the brief, a short process note, the output, the date and location, the collaborators, and any links. Where partner imagery is still being collected, the placeholder is tagged in red.

The Orgasm Project

Sotheby's · EEG · Oil on canvas

EEG recordings of orgasm turned to oil paint, auctioned at Sotheby's for $94,500.

+

A series of EEG-derived paintings made with neuroscientist Abhijeet Satani. One work was auctioned at Sotheby's as an NFT for $94,500.

La petite mort, 2021, oil on canvas, 74 × 74 inches6A

The brief

Neuroscientist Abhijeet Satani approached the studio with a research question: could the EEG signature of an orgasm be turned into a painting that read as art rather than spectacle.

The process

Volunteers' brain activity was recorded during orgasm using EEG. The signal was fed to the SOZO painting machine (developed in the 2015 ArtMatr collaboration) as input. The machine laid initial passes; the studio finished each canvas by hand. La petite mort I to IV were completed in 2021. The Cyborg Orgasm sequence followed in 2022.

Outcome

One work from the series, titled La Petite Mort, was auctioned at Sotheby's as an NFT for $94,500 in 2021. The signal-to-machine-to-hand pipeline established here has been the studio's working method on every series since.

№ 02 · February 2023

Impossible Bouquet 2.0

Gujral Foundation · AI · Acrylic + digital print

AI plus paint plus archival cotton rag. Opened India Art Fair 2023.

+

The second iteration of the Impossible Bouquet series, presented at the Gujral Foundation's 24 Jor Bagh space in New Delhi. Opened India Art Fair week.

Goes the bauble, 2023, acrylic + digital print on archival cotton rag, 43 × 84.6 inches14A

The brief

Feroze Gujral, founder of the Gujral Foundation, commissioned an exhibition to open India Art Fair week. The brief was to engage AI-assisted painting while keeping the materiality of paint.

The process

The Impossible Bouquet draws on the 17th-century Dutch still-life tradition, where painters composed bouquets from flowers that bloomed in different seasons and could not coexist. The studio used AI image generation to compose source compositions, printed them as digital prints on archival cotton rag, and overpainted them with acrylic. Eight works were produced across small and large scales.

Outcome

Eight works installed at 24 Jor Bagh, 9 to 16 February 2023. The show opened India Art Fair week. The curator-led model has since become the studio's default for institutional exhibitions.

№ 03 · 2015 → 2018

SOZO painting machine

ArtMatr · Eric Fischl · Painting robot

A painting robot developed in The Hamptons with ArtMatr and Eric Fischl.

+

A robotic painting collaborator developed during the Eric Fischl studio residency in The Hamptons, with ArtMatr. The Mysterium Tremendum et Fascinans (MTF) series came out of it.

Mysterium Tremendum et Fascinans, 2018, oil on canvasOutput of SOZO collaboration

The brief

Eric Fischl invited the studio to a Hamptons residency. During the residency, ArtMatr, an MIT Media Lab offshoot that builds painting robots, joined the project. The shared question was whether a machine could paint without becoming the subject of the painting.

The process

SOZO is a robotic arm that applies underpaintings based on input from the artist. Across the residency and afterwards, the studio developed a working pipeline: the machine lays first passes, the artist edits and finishes by hand. The Mysterium Tremendum et Fascinans (MTF) paintings were completed in 2018.

Outcome

The Mysterium Tremendum et Fascinans (MTF) series, four oils on canvas, 2018. The human-signal-to-machine-to-hand pipeline established here has been used in every studio AI series since, including The Orgasm Project and Impossible Bouquet 2.0.

Where the Wind Tastes of Forgotten Songs

Royal Enfield · Immersive · Palace install

A site-specific palace installation on the Himalayas, commissioned by Royal Enfield.

+

A site-specific immersive installation commissioned by Royal Enfield, themed around the Himalayas. Combines painting, AI imagery, projection mapping, and original sound design inside a heritage palace setting.

Where the Wind Tastes of Forgotten Songs · Royal Enfield Himalaya project04 · Hero

The brief

Royal Enfield commissioned an installation tied to its Himalayan riding heritage. The brief asked for a work that a non-rider could enter and inhabit, rather than a brand campaign.

The process

The studio developed a multi-sensory environment organised around the geography of the Himalayas rather than the brand's product. Painted panels, AI-generated imagery, projection mapping, and an original sound design were integrated inside a heritage palace setting. Visitors walked through the work as they would a temple or a mountain pass.

Outcome

A walk-through installation, Where the Wind Tastes of Forgotten Songs, presented at the Royal Enfield Art Festival. The place-first method developed here is now the studio's default approach for corporate commissions.

№ 05 · 1999 → 2005

When Paintings Dance

Kamal Haasan · Performance · Watercolour

A year in Kamal Haasan's home; paintings activated by dancers' bodies.

+

A performance-art collaboration with actor and filmmaker Kamal Haasan. Watercolour paintings made directly on leotards worn by dancers; performed in India and the United States with Velocity Circus and Cirque du Soleil performers.

Premiere, Taj West End, Bangalore, 1999 · with Kamal Haasan05 · Hero

The brief

Kamal Haasan invited the studio to develop a performance work that brought painting and dance into the same frame. The studio worked from Haasan's home for nearly a year on the piece.

The process

Watercolour was applied directly onto leotards worn by dancers. The choreography activated the painting; each performance produced a distinct, time-based work. The piece premiered in 1999 at the Taj West End in Bangalore.

Outcome

America India Foundation adopted the piece for its California fundraising gala. Performances followed in San Francisco with Velocity Circus and Cirque du Soleil dancers; the San Francisco exhibition sold out. The work was restaged in 2005 at the San Jose Convention Center under the title "Anthropomorphism: When Paintings Dance."

Album covers, Paul Simon and Erykah Badu

Paul Simon · Erykah Badu · Album design

Album cover work for two musicians, plus a Badu DJ fundraiser.

+

A long-running design collaboration with two musicians. Album cover artwork for Paul Simon and Erykah Badu, plus a hand-designed visual identity for a Badu DJ-fundraiser in New York.

Paul Simon concert backdrop, New York06 · Hero

The brief

Paul Simon and Erykah Badu separately commissioned the studio for visual work tied to their music. With Badu, the relationship later extended to a fundraiser in New York where she would DJ.

Album cover artworks for both musicians were developed as standalone paintings then adapted to record-sleeve format. For the Badu fundraiser, the studio designed T-shirts whose imagery drew on her hair, presence, and politics of self-expression. Badu performed a DJ set at the opening.

The album cover work continued across the 2000s and into the 2010s. Paul Simon has been a long-standing collector of studio paintings.

№ 07 · 2011

Pop-It

Above Solutions · iPad app · Picture book

An iPad book where shaking the device swaps the family on every page.

+

An interactive iPad picture book that uses the accelerometer to change the family on every page.

Pop-It · iPad picture book, accelerometer-driven07 · Hero

The brief

Above Solutions, an interactive-book developer, wanted to use the iPad's accelerometer to make a children's book that paper could not. The studio was asked to design the concept and illustrations.

The process

Pop-It uses a shake-to-change mechanic. The same family appears on every page, but a shake of the device swaps the parents between two fathers, two mothers, or a mother and a father. The child stays the same. Illustrations were hand-drawn and animated for touch.

Outcome

Launched at TED Global. Awarded Kirkus Best Books of 2011 (cited as "a first-rate mind expander") and Best of Singapore Art Fair 2011. The accompanying TED Talk, "Shake up your story," is among the studio's most-viewed talks.

№ 08 · 2013 → 2016

MUST# visual discovery app

Viacom · iOS app · Empathy algorithm

An algorithm built inside Viacom to widen taste instead of confirm it.

+

A visual discovery app built during a Viacom creator-in-residence, designed to widen viewer taste rather than confirm it. Launched on the Apple App Store in 2016.

Launch at TED · with Al Gore08 · Hero

The brief

Viacom hosted the studio as creator-in-residence in New York from 2013 to 2016. The brief was to explore what visual recommendation could look like beyond similarity-based engines. Recommendation systems at the time sorted by likeness; Viacom wanted to know what sorting by expansion would look like.

The process

The studio developed two algorithms with engineering partners. The Visual Empathy Algorithm matched viewers to images outside their stated taste. The Coefficient of Expansion of Empathy measured how far a viewer's preferences could be stretched without losing trust. Both were integrated into a single iOS app, MUST#.

Outcome

MUST# launched on the Apple App Store in 2016. Premiered at TED and Viacom HQ. The two-algorithm framework was later re-used in The Orgasm Project (entry № 01) and Impossible Bouquet 2.0 (entry № 02).

№ 09 · 2020

Eye Candy

Under25 Studios · Instagram · Performance

A continuous performance work staged on Instagram during lockdown.

+

A serial performance-art project staged on Instagram during the COVID-19 lockdown, produced with Under25 Studios. Seven oil paintings entered the studio's archive from this series.

Balance, 2020, oil on canvas, 48 × 36 inches10A

The brief

COVID-19 lockdowns closed galleries. Under25 Studios, a youth-focused media platform in India, asked the studio whether a serious painting practice could happen entirely on Instagram without collapsing into marketing.

The process

Eye Candy was structured as a continuous work rather than a sequence of posts. The studio produced seven oil paintings, fourteen carousel posts, ten mini-podcasts, seven exhibition videos, and four live sessions over the run. The feed was treated as a venue; carousels as walls; live sessions as openings.

Outcome

Three of the paintings, Balance, Conflict, and Personality, entered the Studio archive. The project remains the studio's reference case for platform-native art when given time.

INK Fellows program

INK Conference · Fellowship · India

Founding and running India's premier multidisciplinary fellowship for a decade.

+

A multidisciplinary fellowship program for early-career thinkers in India, run by INK Conference.

Co-chair, 2015 · with Lalitesh Katragadda10 · Hero

The brief

INK Conference, India's TED-format platform, wanted a fellowship for early-career thinkers across disciplines. Models like TED Fellows existed elsewhere, but not in India.

The process

Founded the program around 2010 and chaired it solo for the first five years. From 2015, co-chaired it with Lalitesh Katragadda (founder, Google Map Maker) and Anu Sethuram (co-founder, INK). Each cohort was curated annually across fields, science, design, performance, media, and matched with mentors. The December 2011 Jaipur edition was the first major showcase.

Outcome

Roughly ten cohorts over a decade. Alumni include founders, researchers, and creative directors. The program is widely cited as India's premier multidisciplinary fellowship.

Fidelio, a brainwave-driven conductor

Visva Subbaraman · EEG · Projection

A conductor's EEG translated into projected imagery during Beethoven's Fidelio.

+

A music-and-neuroscience collaboration with conductor Vishwa Subbaraman. EEG-derived signal from the conductor's brain activity was integrated into a performance of Beethoven's Fidelio at the Skylight Music Theatre, Milwaukee.

Skylight Music Theatre, Milwaukee · brainwave performance11 · Hero

The brief

Conductor Vishwa Subbaraman approached the studio about a piece that would visualise the inner concentration of a conductor in real time during a live performance.

The process

EEG sensors recorded Subbaraman's brain activity during the performance of Beethoven's Fidelio. The studio built a visual system that translated the EEG signal into projected imagery synchronised with the music. The piece was staged at the Skylight Music Theatre, Milwaukee.

Outcome

The technical pipeline, brainwave signal to visual output, fed directly into The Orgasm Project (entry № 01), where EEG was again the input layer.

№ 12 · 2018 → 2020

Story Collective

Jay Ahia · Residency · India

Five residencies in Rajasthan and Goa, practitioners from unrelated fields.

+

A multidisciplinary residency program co-created with Jay Ahia, held five times in Rajasthan and Goa before COVID-19 paused the program.

Placeholder
Placeholder, awaiting residency photography5A · Catch 'em

The brief

India lacked a recurring residency that put practitioners from unrelated disciplines, writers, designers, technologists, dancers, scientists, in one room long enough to collaborate. Jay Ahia (founder, The Experience Company / BHX) and the studio co-created the format.

The process

Each edition ran for about a week. Roughly twenty practitioners were invited per edition, given a shared prompt, and asked to collaborate across disciplines. Workshops, demos, and shared meals filled the schedule. The workshop method "Kill Your Talk, Not Your Audience" was developed during these editions.

Outcome

Three editions in Rajasthan, two in Goa. COVID-19 paused the program in 2020. Alumni continue to collaborate independently. The workshop format is still used by the studio in mentorship work.

Robin Hood Foundation library

Robin Hood Foundation · Pentagram · Library design

A New York school library, designed with Pentagram.

+

A school library in New York, commissioned by the Robin Hood Foundation and designed in partnership with Pentagram. The studio contributed the artwork that holds the room.

Robin Hood Foundation library, New York15 · Hero

The brief

The Robin Hood Foundation, a New York anti-poverty foundation, funds school libraries in under-resourced city schools. Pentagram led the design programme. The studio was invited to contribute the artwork that would give one of those libraries its character.

The process

The studio worked alongside Pentagram on a library where the art was not decoration on the wall but part of the room's architecture: the surfaces children touched, the shelves they walked between, the visual logic of a place built to make them want to stay.

Outcome

A finished, in-use library in a New York public school, documented by The Architect's Newspaper. The Robin Hood / Pentagram library programme has gone on to commission dozens more rooms in the same model.

Gradient Descent

Nature Morte · AI · Curation

The first gallery exhibition built around AI-generated art.

+

A curated group show built around AI-generated artwork, presented at Nature Morte gallery, New Delhi. Cited as the first show of its kind inside a gallery.

Gradient Descent · install at Nature Morte16 · Hero

The brief

Nature Morte, the New Delhi contemporary gallery, gave the studio space to curate a show that took AI-generated work seriously as art, rather than as novelty.

The process

The studio assembled artists and works that used generative models as part of their making, organised them so the technology disappeared into the question each piece was asking, and wrote the framing for visitors who had never seen the form before.

Outcome

Cited by artnet as the first AI-as-art exhibition staged in a gallery. The framework — AI inside the room, not on the press release — has since shaped the studio's own AI shows, including Impossible Bouquet 2.0.

Ricky Kej · UN COP21 · Album

Artistic producer on a 300-musician album launched at the UN Climate Conference.

+

Artistic producer on Shanti Samsara, a global music album led by Grammy winner Ricky Kej. Launched at the 2015 United Nations Climate Change Conference in Paris by Prime Minister Narendra Modi and President François Hollande.

Composer Ricky Kej, a Grammy winner, was assembling a record that would tie music to climate as the United Nations met in Paris for COP21. The brief to the studio was artistic direction: what does an album about the earth look like when it has 300 musicians on it.

Mona Lisa 2.0

Brainwave art · EEG · Series

An EEG-driven reading of the Mona Lisa, painted from brain signal.

+

A series of paintings produced from EEG-recorded responses to Leonardo's Mona Lisa. Brainwave signal becomes the input layer; the resulting compositions are the painting.

Mona Lisa 2.0 · brainwave art series19 · Hero

The brief

If a painting can be made from a conductor's brain (entry № 11, Fidelio) and an orgasm (entry № 01, The Orgasm Project), what does it look like to make one from the act of looking at the most-looked-at painting in the world. Mona Lisa 2.0 was the studio's working answer.

The process

EEG recordings of a viewer responding to Leonardo's Mona Lisa were translated into compositional input for paint. The resulting works are not portraits of the Mona Lisa; they are portraits of the act of seeing her.

Outcome

A multi-panel series in the brainwave-art lineage. Details TK.

64 by 1.

An art collective co-founded with Karthik Kalyanaraman in 2018, built to blur the lines between art, art criticism, and art education. Featured on the cover of Marg for the magazine's 75th anniversary issue. Strange Genders, an AI-art collaboration with Harshit Agrawal under the 64/1 banner, was longlisted for the Lumen Prize 2021 and shown in Exostential at the Emami Art Foundation, Kolkata.

Karthik Kalyanaraman and Raghava KK, co-founders64/1 · Founders

What it is

64/1 is a cultural collective for the future, co-founded in 2018 by Karthik Kalyanaraman and the studio. The name is taken from the chess problem of the grain on the chessboard — each square doubling — and is a working frame for how ideas, criticism, and education can compound when held in the same place.

What it does

The collective produces artworks, criticism, and educational programming as a single body of work. Strange Genders, with Harshit Agrawal, is the collective's first published AI-art piece — a collaborative work exhibited as part of Exostential at the Emami Art Foundation, Kolkata, and longlisted for the Lumen Prize 2021.

Where it has been

India's leading culture magazine Marg ran 64/1 on the cover of its 75th anniversary issue, framing the collective as a trailblazer of the next generation of Indian cultural practice.
