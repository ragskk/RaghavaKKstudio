How to handle them

The shelves hold a mix of works. Pantheon pieces — caricatured figures of contemporary power — sit alongside softer studio dolls and one holographic toy. Each one is shown as a three-dimensional model: drag a toy to turn it, let go to watch it rotate on its own. The shelves only animate the toys you are looking at; the rest sit still until you reach them. Hover any toy to read its year, medium, and a short note above the shelf.
