Field №01A studio experiment

A drifting network, redrawn on every visit.

Around two dozen paintings, sampled at random from across the series, set to drift like a slow flock. The lines are decorative. They wire and rewire by proximity, in graphite. Nothing is being argued. The work is to sit with more than one thing being true at once, and to watch the configurations the room keeps offering.
