the case for an archive that contradicts itself.
handbook v1.1 · april 2026
i, most archives

Most artist archives protect a narrative.

They curate what is visible and bury what is inconvenient. This one does the opposite. It holds the verified public claim alongside the private critique of that claim, the achievement and the exaggeration, the vision and the ego, the dharma and the hunger that drove it.

ii, credibility

Vulnerability is the engine.

The institution's credibility comes from what it is willing to admit, not what it claims. A record that holds its own contradictions is more trustworthy than one that only shows achievements. The weakness is the strength. The honesty is the long-term value.

iii, coherence

The multiplicity is the method.

Twenty-five years of work across wildly different mediums, paint, ceramic, hologram, code, looks scattered to outsiders. The archive shows that it is not. Every work belongs to threads. Every thread has a dharmic essence. Pluralism is a practice, not a position.

iv, for the long view

What you are actually engaging with.

Not an object. A node in a verified, living system of thought that will continue to grow in meaning long after you encounter it. The institution is upstream of every gallery, every collector, every press notice. This is the source.
