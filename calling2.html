Calling All Gods.

An open call to deities, addressable entities, and any belief structure currently in operational use.

Parsons · New York December 2026
Vishwaroopa Plate i
Two Paintings Together Plate ii
§ 1The Call

The studio of Raghava KK is now accepting submissions from gods, demigods, prophets, prophets-once-removed, idols, household spirits, ancestral attendants, animal incarnations, syncretic composites, brand mascots, algorithmic intermediaries, AI agents currently being addressed in supplication, and any other belief structure presently in operational use.

Self-nomination encouraged. Theological legitimacy is not a disqualifying criterion.

§ 2Eligibility

Candidates may be of any cosmology, any age, any vintage. Multi-armed welcome. Single-attribute welcome. Recently invented welcome. Long-retired welcome. Operational status, currency of worship, and consensus across traditions are not required.

Submission is open to entities being addressed by anyone, anywhere, in any tongue, with any frequency.

§ 3Review Criteria
  • i.Iconographic durability The face survives reproduction.
  • ii.Emotional load-bearing Holds weight when addressed sincerely.
  • iii.Cross-cultural portability Travels without losing identity.
  • iv.Retrievability under stress Comes when called.
  • v.Visibility under contemporary lighting Legible to a 2026 audience.

§ 5Director's Note · Draft

The title is meant straight. Pluralism in 2026 is no longer a question of whether there are many gods, there are, and most of them are working. The question is which ones are currently on call, who is dialing, and what infrastructure mediates the connection.

Calling All Gods is an open invitation to whatever is currently being addressed in the world. The premise of the show is that addressability, not authenticity, is the contemporary criterion of divinity. A god is what gets called.[Replace with your own. The above is a stub.]
