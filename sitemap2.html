A working map of every room.

The studio's pages, drawn as a small drifting network. Hover any room to read what's inside. The red pulses are signals passing between rooms: when a painting is in conversation with a toy, with an essay, with an archive note, this is what it looks like.

fig. 01 · live · pencil on paper · hover any room

How to read it

The black node at the centre is the index. Around it, the five primary rooms: studio, lab, calling all gods, becoming archived, excavate. Further out, the secondary pages: about, writings, the field experiment, the terms. Lines are routes, drawn in graphite. A red pulse traveling along a line is the simplest way to say that one room is currently in conversation with another. Click any room to enter it.
