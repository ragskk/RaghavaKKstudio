№04India's first hybrid art show, made natively on Instagram

Eye Candy.

A project · with Under25 Studios · 2020 → 2021

A show whose venue was the grid itself, run through the lockdown months. Spoken essays to camera, paint filmed at skin distance, and streamed lives in between. The lives are gone — that was the wager of a show made of appearances. What remains plays here, from this site, not from Meta.
